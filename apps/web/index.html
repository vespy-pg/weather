FORECAST LEGEND

How to read this forecast

Temperature line

White: deep frost at -12°C or below. Gray: frost below 0°C. Blue: cold from 0°C to 18°C. Green: comfortable from 18°C to 27°C. Orange: hot from 27°C to 32°C. Red: extreme heat above 32°C.

Feels-like area

The shaded area between the lines shows the difference from measured temperature. Blue means it feels colder, orange means it feels warmer.

Sun, clouds and night

Yellow glow is exposed sunlight and fades as cloud cover grows. The gray band becomes larger with more cloud. A moon marks nighttime.

Precipitation - one symbol per hour

💧 rain, ❄ snow, ◆ hail. Symbol size shows the amount. Opacity shows probability.

⚡Thunderstorm

Lightning appears on the temperature chart. A larger, brighter bolt means a more probable and stronger storm.

↑Wind

The arrow points in the wind direction. Faded gray means calm, then green means light, yellow means strong, and red means 30 km/h or more. The number is speed in km/h.
