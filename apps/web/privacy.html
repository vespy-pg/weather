Weather privacy policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026

Weather provides forecasts, historical weather context, saved locations, and configurable home-screen widgets. No account is required.

Location and forecast requests

You can search for a place or optionally grant access to your device's approximate location. The selected place name and coordinates are sent over HTTPS to the Weather service at api.weather.vespy.eu so it can return forecasts and resolve location names. The service sends coordinates and forecast parameters to Open-Meteo. Reverse location lookup uses OpenStreetMap Nominatim. If you enable mushroom conditions, nearby observation requests may also be sent to iNaturalist.

Saved places, widget choices, display preferences, and the active location are stored locally on your device. They are removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

Optional analytics

Firebase Analytics is disabled by default. If you explicitly allow analytics, the app records feature interactions such as forecast loads, settings changes, and promotion views or clicks. It does not include searched place names, coordinates, or weather search text in analytics events. Advertising storage, advertising user data, and ad personalization remain disabled. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings.

Server logs

Infrastructure providers may process IP addresses, request URLs, device user-agent information, timestamps, and error details in access or security logs. Request URLs can include a selected place or coordinates. These logs are used only to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service and are retained according to operational log rotation.

Promotions and external links

The app may display first-party promotions for other Vespy products. Opening a promotion sends you to the linked website, which has its own privacy practices. Weather does not use an advertising SDK.

Security and sharing

Network requests use HTTPS. Data is disclosed only to service providers needed to deliver forecasts, location lookup, optional observations, analytics you consent to, hosting, and security. Weather does not sell personal data.

Your choices

  • Do not grant location permission and search for a place manually.
  • Decline or disable optional analytics in Settings.
  • Remove saved places in Settings.
  • Clear app storage or uninstall the app to remove locally stored data.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email webmaster@vespy.eu.
