# Google Play App Content draft

## Store setup

- App name: Vespy Weather
- Default language: English (United States)
- App or game: App
- Category: Weather
- Contact email: webmaster@vespy.eu
- Privacy policy URL: https://weather.vespy.eu/privacy.html

## Declarations

- App access: All functionality is available without login or membership.
- Ads: Select Yes while first-party Vespy promotional cards are present. The app does not use an advertising SDK.
- Target audience: Designed for a general audience aged 13 and older. Do not select child age groups unless Families Policy compliance is reviewed separately.
- News app: No.
- Government app: No.
- Financial features: None.
- Health features: None.
- User-generated content: None.
- Social features: None.
- In-app purchases: None.
- Content rating: Complete the questionnaire as a utility app with no violence, sexual content, gambling, controlled substances or user communication.

## Permissions

- INTERNET: Required for forecast, place search, promotions and optional analytics.
- ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: Optional. Used only after the user chooses Use device location.
- No background location, precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage, SMS or call-log permission.

## Tester notes

- No credentials are required.
- Declining analytics does not restrict functionality.
- To test without location permission, search for any city or postal code.
- Demo data is available within the application where labelled.
- Test the resizable home-screen widget separately from the main application.
